## Out (must-write paths / words)

- `loop-design/NOTE.md` with three sections:
  - `## Craft` — observe → choose → act → verify → record → stop (success / no-op / blocked / approval / exhausted).
  - `## Audit` — Loop-doctor word: `Ready` | `Repair needed` | `Not actually a loop`.
  - `## Debrief` — proposals only (paths under `proposals/` if you write patches). Do not apply them.
- Exactly one audit word from that list in the note.

## Must-not

- Run product work, edit `src/` / owned files, or author `.wm/FALSIFIER`.
- Be the delivery walker: do not `wm loop` slices, do not replace `wm.sh`.
- Publish, find, or adapt from a Loop Library (no catalog URLs, no external publish).
- Enable a schedule, background farm, or host-skill weld.
- Treat one toy run as a pattern that rewrites the engine.
